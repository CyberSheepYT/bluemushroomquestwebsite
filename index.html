How it plays

Gather. Brew. Become the Master Brewer.

A cozy loop with a little treasure hunt hiding inside it.

01

Gather

Wander a forest that's 9,000 by 7,000 steps wide and fill your backpack with coffee beans, Camellia sinensis and flowers. Each one has its own quality.

02

Brew

Bring three ingredients of the same kind to the brew station. Better ingredients make a better drink, and every cup gets a score out of 100.

03

Collect

Your recipe book writes itself. Track down the 7 hidden legendary ingredients, tucked behind bushes in the farthest corners, and find the secret recipe.

Every cup is scored

Better beans,
better brews.

Your drink's score is the average quality of the three ingredients you used. The common ones are fine. The legendary ones (93 and up) hide far from home.

Day & night

When night falls, light your lantern.

Days and nights last ten minutes each. Sleep in your tent until morning, or stay up and follow the fireflies. They like to hang around hidden ingredients. The weather changes on its own, too.

Lantern Fireflies Rain Fog Wind Campfire
The world

Five areas, one huge forest.

Each part of the forest grows different ingredients and has a mood of its own. Start at home in the middle and head out in any direction.

Co-op

Bring your friends.

Play together on your home network or online. Everyone gets their own backpack and recipe book, picks a cap colour, and can chat by pressing T.
